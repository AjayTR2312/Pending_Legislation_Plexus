Rule 1: BILL HEADER FORMAT (Page 1 Only)
⚠️ CRITICAL: The page 1 header MUST follow this EXACT line-by-line structure:

Line 2: (blank)

🚨 COMMON ERROR: Putting "General Assembly" and bill number on the SAME line.
❌ WRONG: "General Assembly Raised Bill No. 6849" (all on one line)
❌ WRONG: "General Assembly            Raised Bill No. 6849" (same line with spaces)
✅ CORRECT:
   Raised Bill No. 6849

   General Assembly

The bill number and "General Assembly" MUST be on SEPARATE lines.

Rule 2: SECTION NUMBERING
Connecticut uses "Sec." with numbers:
Sec. 1. (a) Text here...      [PRESERVE - section header]
Sec. 2. Text here...          [PRESERVE - section header]

Rule 3: SUBSECTION HIERARCHY
(a) First level               [PRESERVE - with blank line after]
  (1) Second level            [PRESERVE - indented]
    (A) Third level           [PRESERVE - further indented]

Rule 4: STATUTE REFERENCES
Connecticut General Statutes format:
- "section 10-145b"           [PRESERVE exact format]
- "subsection (a) of section 10-16p"  [PRESERVE]
- NEVER change format (e.g., don't make "Section 10-145b")

Rule 5: ENACTING CLAUSE
Standard Connecticut format (always preserve):
"Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Assembly convened:"

Rule 6: TAGS WITH BRACKETS
Connecticut uses brackets in [D> tags to show legislative notation:
[D>[text]<D]                  [PRESERVE brackets inside tag]
NOT: [D>text<D]               [WRONG - brackets removed]

Rule 7: TEXT REFLOW
Connecticut bills often have artificial line breaks:
BEFORE:
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of
Representatives in General Assembly convened:

AFTER:
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Assembly convened:

Rule 8: BLANK LINES BETWEEN SECTIONS
After each section, use 1 blank line:
Sec. 1. (a) Text here...
                              [1 blank line]
Sec. 2. Text here...

Rule 9: SUBSECTION SPACING
After each subsection like (a), (b), (c), use 1 blank line:
(a) Text for subsection a...
                              [1 blank line]
(b) Text for subsection b...

Rule 10: EFFECTIVE DATE FORMAT
Connecticut commonly uses:
"Effective October 1, 2025"
"Effective from passage"
"Effective July 1, 2025, and applicable to..."
[PRESERVE exact format - usually at end of bill]
