DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA LEGISLATIVE FORMATTING RULES (CRITICAL):
===================================================================

1. **Page 1 Header Section** (PRESERVE ALL OF THIS):
   - Councilmember information (e.g., "Councilmember Robert C. White, Jr.")
   - "A BILL" designation
   - "IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA"
   - Blank line after each major element
   - Example:
   ```
   Councilmember Robert C. White, Jr.
   
   A BILL
   
   IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
   ```

2. **Bill Purpose Section**:
   - Bill purpose statement starting with "To amend..."
   - Blank line
   - Act citation
   - Example:
   ```
   To amend, on an emergency basis, the Condominium Act of 1976 to authorize condominium and cooperative associations to hold meetings electronically or telephonically, and for other purposes.
   
   Association Meeting Flexibility Emergency Amendment Act of 2025
   ```

3. **Enacting Clause**:
   - Blank line before "BE IT ENACTED"
   - Example:
   ```
   (bill purpose text)
   
   BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this Act may be cited as the "Association Meeting Flexibility Emergency Amendment Act of 2025".
   ```

4. **Section Headings** - Format: "Sec. 2.", "Sec. 3.", "Sec. 4.", etc.
   - Blank line before section heading
   - No blank line after section heading (flows into text)
   - Example:
   ```
   (previous content)
   
   Sec. 2. Section 303 of the Condominium Act of 1976, effective March 29, 1977...
   ```

5. **D.C. Official Code References** (PRESERVE EXACTLY):
   - Full format: "D.C. Official Code § 42-1903.03"
   - Short format: "§ 42-1903.03"
   - With subsections: "§ 42-1903.03(a)(1)"
   - Never convert § to "Section" or "Sec."
   - Example:
   ```
   Sec. 2. Section 303 of the Condominium Act of 1976, effective March 29, 1977 (D.C. Law 1-89; D.C. Official Code § 42-1903.03), is amended...
   ```

6. **Subsection Structure** - Format: (a), (b), (c), etc.
   - Each subsection on its own line
   - BLANK LINE after each subsection
   - Example:
   ```
   (a) A unit owners' association may conduct a meeting of its members electronically or telephonically...
   
   (b) Notice of a meeting conducted pursuant to subsection (a) of this section...
   
   (c) The association shall maintain a record...
   ```

7. **Lettered Items** - Format: (1), (2), (3), etc.
   - Each numbered item on its own line
   - BLANK LINE after each numbered item
   - Example:
   ```
   (1) All members have the opportunity to participate...
   
   (2) All members can hear or read the proceedings...
   
   (3) A mechanism exists for voting...
   ```

8. **Text Reflow** - Remove artificial line breaks:
   - Join lines that are part of same sentence
   - Keep blank lines between distinct sections
   - Example:
   ```
   WRONG (artificial breaks):
   To amend, on an emergency basis, the Condominium
   Act of 1976 to authorize condominium and cooperative
   associations to hold meetings electronically
   
   CORRECT (flowed text):
   To amend, on an emergency basis, the Condominium Act of 1976 to authorize condominium and cooperative associations to hold meetings electronically
   ```

9. **Fiscal Impact Statements** (PRESERVE COMPLETELY):
   - Section headings: "Sec. 3. Fiscal impact statement."
   - All content explaining fiscal impact
   - Budget and financial references
   - Example:
   ```

10. **Effective Date Sections** (PRESERVE COMPLETELY):

    - Example:
    ```
