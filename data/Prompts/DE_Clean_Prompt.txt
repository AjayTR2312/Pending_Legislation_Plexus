LINE NUMBERS - MUST BE REMOVED (All Pages)
===================================================================

⚠️ CRITICAL: LINE NUMBERS ARE ARTIFACTS - REMOVE FROM ALL PAGES

WHAT ARE LINE NUMBERS?
Delaware PDF extraction adds sequential numbers at the beginning of lines:
- Format: Usually 1-2 spaces followed by number, then 1-2 more spaces
- Examples: "  1  ", "  3  ", "  5  ", "  7  ", "  8  ", " 10  ", " 12  ", " 15  ", " 19  ", " 24  ", " 31  ", " 37  "
- These appear at the start of lines throughout the document
- They are NOT part of the legislative text

EXAMPLES OF LINE NUMBERS TO REMOVE:
```
 1       Section 1. Amend § 6052, Chapter 60A, Title 29
 3       § 6052. Definitions.
 5       (5) "Maximum annual match" means the maximum match
 7       (6) "Maximum match" means $20 from and after
 8       (5) (7) "Qualified participant" is defined as
10            a. Employee must be enrolled in the deferred
```

AFTER REMOVING LINE NUMBERS:
```
Section 1. Amend § 6052, Chapter 60A, Title 29
§ 6052. Definitions.
(5) "Maximum annual match" means the maximum match
(6) "Maximum match" means $20 from and after
(5) (7) "Qualified participant" is defined as
     a. Employee must be enrolled in the deferred
```

⚠️ SAFE LINE NUMBER REMOVAL PROCESS:

1. IDENTIFY line numbers:
   - Look for 1-3 digit numbers at the start of lines
   - Usually have spaces before and after the number
   - Pattern: "  1  ", "  3  ", " 10  ", " 24  ", etc.

2. BEFORE REMOVING: Read the text AFTER the line number
   - Example: "  7       (6) 'Maximum match' means $20 from and after"
   - Text after number: "(6) 'Maximum match' means $20 from and after"
   - COPY mentally: This text must be preserved

3. REMOVE ONLY the line number and spacing before the text
   - Delete: "  7       " (number + spaces)
   - Keep: "(6) 'Maximum match' means $20 from and after"

4. IMMEDIATELY VERIFY:
   - Is the text after the line number still present?
   - Did you only remove the number, not the legislative text?
   - Example: "(6) 'Maximum match' means $20 from and after" ✓ PRESENT

5. SPECIAL CASE: Indented text with line numbers
   - Input: " 10            a. Employee must be enrolled"
   - The spaces AFTER the line number are INTENTIONAL INDENTATION
   - Remove: " 10  " (line number + immediate spaces)
   - Keep: "          a. Employee must be enrolled" (indentation + text)
   - Output: "          a. Employee must be enrolled"

⚠️ CRITICAL: Do NOT remove subsection numbers like (1), (2), (3), a., b., c.
These are LEGISLATIVE STRUCTURE, not line numbers!

EXAMPLE - DISTINGUISH LINE NUMBERS FROM SUBSECTION NUMBERS:
```
 5       (5) "Maximum annual match" means the maximum match
```
- " 5       " = LINE NUMBER (remove this)
- "(5)" = SUBSECTION NUMBER (keep this)
- Output: "(5) "Maximum annual match" means the maximum match"

   a. BEFORE JOINING: Read the COMPLETE sentence (all lines)

   c. Join the lines by removing line breaks
   d. IMMEDIATELY verify: Count words in result vs original
   e. If word count decreased → Text was dropped, RESTORE IT

4. Reduce excessive blank lines:
   a. This is SAFE (blank lines don't contain content)
   b. Just ensure you're not removing blank lines that separate sections
