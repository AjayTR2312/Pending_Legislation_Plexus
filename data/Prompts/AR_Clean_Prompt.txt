APPROPRIATION TABLES — STRIP DECORATIVE [A>...<A] TAGS
===================================================================

⚠️ CRITICAL FOR AR APPROPRIATION BILLS:

Arkansas appropriation bills (those with "AN ACT TO MAKE AN APPROPRIATION…")
contain large salary/budget tables.  In these tables, the PDF uses
**underline as typographic emphasis**, NOT as a legislative-addition marker.
The extractor cannot tell the two apart, so it wraps decorative table
content with `[A>…<A]` tags by mistake.

🚫 STRIP THE [A>…<A] WRAPPER (keep the inside text) when ALL of these hold:

1. **The line is part of an appropriation table or schedule**, AND
2. **The tag wraps one of these three patterns**:

   PATTERN A — Column-header row (table column titles):
   ❌ WRONG: `[A> No.     Title     Employees     2025-2026<A]`
   ✅ RIGHT: ` No.     Title     Employees     2025-2026`

   ❌ WRONG: `[A> NO.                                       2025-2026<A]`
   ✅ RIGHT: ` NO.                                       2025-2026`

   PATTERN B — All-caps category heading row (no lowercase letters):
   ❌ WRONG: `[A>TWELVE MONTH EDUCATIONAL AND GENERAL ADMINISTRATIVE POSITIONS<A]`
   ✅ RIGHT: `TWELVE MONTH EDUCATIONAL AND GENERAL ADMINISTRATIVE POSITIONS`

   ❌ WRONG: `[A>NINE MONTH EDUCATIONAL AND GENERAL ACADEMIC POSITIONS<A]`
   ✅ RIGHT: `NINE MONTH EDUCATIONAL AND GENERAL ACADEMIC POSITIONS`

   ❌ WRONG: `[A>TWELVE MONTH AUXILIARY ENTERPRISES POSITIONS<A]`
   ✅ RIGHT: `TWELVE MONTH AUXILIARY ENTERPRISES POSITIONS`

   PATTERN C — Bare dollar amount in an appropriation schedule row
   (the tag wraps ONLY a number/dollar value, no surrounding prose):
   ❌ WRONG: `(05) CONTINGENCY                          [A>      573,644<A]`
   ✅ RIGHT: `(05) CONTINGENCY                                573,644`

   ❌ WRONG: `      TOTAL AMOUNT APPROPRIATED            [A> $12,046,523<A]`
   ✅ RIGHT: `      TOTAL AMOUNT APPROPRIATED              $12,046,523`

   ❌ WRONG: `(05) DEBT SERVICE                         [A>    2,200,000<A]`
   ✅ RIGHT: `(05) DEBT SERVICE                              2,200,000`

✅ KEEP THE [A>…<A] WRAPPER UNTOUCHED in these cases (these are REAL changes):

   COUNTEREXAMPLE 1 — Tag wraps prose / a phrase, not just a number:
   ✅ KEEP: `SECTION 1. Arkansas Code Title 5 is [A>amended to add an additional section to read<A] as follows:`
   (The tag wraps a verb phrase — that is a real legislative addition.)

   COUNTEREXAMPLE 2 — Tag wraps a complete new section/clause:
   ✅ KEEP: `[A>15-4-221.  Report concerning contracts awarded to businesses.<A]`
   (The tag wraps a brand-new code section being inserted — keep it.)

   COUNTEREXAMPLE 3 — Tag wraps an inline addition mixed with regular text:
   ✅ KEEP: `(2) IT Pool [A>15<A] employees`
   (Here the bill is genuinely changing the employee count to 15 — keep it.
    But if the line were just `(2) IT Pool [A>15<A]` with the number sitting
    in the right-hand "Employees" column of an appropriation table, that's
    Pattern C above and the wrapper IS stripped.)

🔑 RULE OF THUMB: If the [A>…<A] sits on a line that is clearly **a row of
   a salary/appropriation table or its column/category header** AND wraps
   ONLY column-title text, all-caps category text, or a bare numeric/dollar
   value, strip the wrapper.  Otherwise KEEP it untouched.
