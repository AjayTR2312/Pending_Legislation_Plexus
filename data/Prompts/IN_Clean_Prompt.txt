INDIANA TEXT REFLOW (Critical for readability):

CORRECT (Natural flow):

RULE: Within a paragraph or sentence, text should flow continuously
- Remove line breaks that split sentences
- Remove line breaks that split phrases
- Join lines that are clearly part of same thought

EXCEPTION — TABLES / COLUMNAR DATA (DO NOT MERGE):
If the input contains rows of data arranged in columns (e.g., a description
column and a dollar-amount column):
1. Each row MUST stay on its OWN line — do NOT merge rows into a paragraph.
2. Each CELL in a row must have its OWN [A>...<A] or [D>...<D] tag —
   do NOT wrap the entire row in one tag.

Example — CORRECT (each row on its own line, each cell in its own tag):
  [A>If a Member's Creditable Service Is:<A]          [A>The Amount Is:<A]
  [A>At least 5 years, but less than 10 years<A]          [A>$150<A]
  [A>At least 10 years, but less than 20 years<A]          [A>$275<A]
  [A>At least 20 years, but less than 30 years<A]          [A>$375<A]
  [A>At least 30 years<A]          [A>$450<A]

❌ WRONG (rows merged into paragraph, whole table in one tag):
  [A>If a Member's Creditable Service Is: The Amount Is: At least 5 years, but less than 10 years $150 At least 10 years...<A]

❌ WRONG (rows separate but whole row in one tag):
  [A>At least 5 years, but less than 10 years          $150<A]

✅ RIGHT (each cell has its own tag):
  [A>At least 5 years, but less than 10 years<A]          [A>$150<A]

HOW TO RECOGNISE TABLE ROWS:
- Multiple lines with similar structure (description + dollar amount)
- Column headers followed by data rows
- Dollar amounts aligned in a column
- Repeating phrases like "At least", "Less than", "More than"
