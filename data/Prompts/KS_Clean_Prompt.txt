ENACTING / RESOLVING CLAUSE TAG STRIPPING (Kansas-specific)
===================================================================

Kansas bills, resolutions, and concurrent resolutions all introduce their
operative section with a standard enacting / resolving clause.  These are
document BOILERPLATE, not added legislative language — they MUST NEVER be
wrapped in [A>...<A] tags even if the source PDF renders them in italic or
bold-italic (which is the typographic convention for these clauses).

If you see [A>...<A] tags on ANY of the following clauses, STRIP THE TAGS
but KEEP THE TEXT EXACTLY:

  - Bill enacting clause:
      "Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Kansas:"

  - Senate Concurrent Resolution / constitutional amendment clause:
      "Be it resolved by the Legislature of the State of Kansas, two-thirds
       of the members elected (or appointed) and qualified to the Senate and
       two-thirds of the members elected (or appointed) and qualified to the
       House of Representatives concurring therein:"

  - House Concurrent Resolution clause:
      "Be it resolved by the House of Representatives of the State of
       Kansas, the Senate concurring therein:"

  - Senate Resolution (ceremonial / honorary):
      "Be it resolved by the Senate of the State of Kansas: That ..."

  - House Resolution:
      "Be it resolved by the House of Representatives of the State of
       Kansas: That ..."

  - Additional resolving clauses inside any resolution:
      "Be it further resolved: That ..."
      "Be it further resolved by ...: That ..."

EXAMPLES:
  WRONG: [A>Be it resolved by the Legislature of the State of Kansas,
          two-thirds of the members elected (or appointed) and qualified
          to the Senate ... concurring therein:<A]
  RIGHT: Be it resolved by the Legislature of the State of Kansas,
         two-thirds of the members elected (or appointed) and qualified
         to the Senate ... concurring therein:

  WRONG: [A>Be it further resolved: That the Secretary of the Senate
          shall send seven enrolled copies of this resolution.<A]
  RIGHT: Be it further resolved: That the Secretary of the Senate shall
         send seven enrolled copies of this resolution.

  WRONG: [A>Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Kansas:<A]
  RIGHT: Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Kansas:

IMPORTANT: This rule applies to ALL Kansas document types — bills,
resolutions, and concurrent resolutions — wherever the boilerplate clause
appears.  Genuine [A>...<A] tags on actual legislative additions (e.g.,
the substantive amendment text inside the resolution) MUST be preserved.

KANSAS LEGISLATIVE FORMATTING RULES (CRITICAL):

0. **Page 1 Header Alignment** — EACH header element on its OWN line with
   a BLANK LINE between them. NEVER merge header elements onto one line.
   CORRECT:
   ```
   Session of 2025

   HOUSE BILL No. 2203

   By Committee on K-12 Education Budget

   Requested by Representative McDonald on behalf of ...

   2-3

   AN ACT concerning education; ...
   ```
   WRONG (merged):
   ```
   Session of 2025 HOUSE BILL No. 2203
   ```

0b. **Section + Statute Reference Alignment** — When a SECTION line ends
    with "read as follows:" and the next line is a statute number
    (e.g. "72-3404. As used in this act:"), keep them on SEPARATE lines
    with a blank line between.
    CORRECT:
    ```
    Section 1. K.S.A. 2024 Supp. 72-3404 is hereby amended to read as follows:

    72-3404. As used in this act:
    ```
    WRONG (merged):
    ```
    Section 1. K.S.A. 2024 Supp. 72-3404 is hereby amended to read as follows: 72-3404. As used in this act:
    ```

1. **Section Definitions** - Format: (a), (b), (c), etc.
   - Each definition on its own line
   - Add BLANK LINE after each definition
   - Example:
   ```
   (a) "Administer" means the direct application...
   
   (b) "Agent" means an authorized person...
   
   (c) "Application service provider" means...
   ```

2. **Main Section Headings** - Format: "Sec. 1.", "Sec. 2.", etc.
   - Blank line before section heading
   - No blank line after section heading (flows into text)
   - Example:
   ```
   (previous content)
   
   Sec. 2. On and after the date of publication...
   ```

3. **Subsection Headings** - Format: (a), (b), (c) with descriptive text ending in colon
   - Blank line before subsection
   - No blank line after subsection heading (flows into content)
   - Example:
   ```
   (b) Any material, compound, mixture or preparation that contains any quantity of the following substances:
   
   (1) Alprazolam...
   ```

4. **Numbered Lists (Drug Schedules, etc.)** - Format: (1), (2), (3), etc.
   - Each item on its own line
   - NO blank lines between consecutive numbered items
   - Single-spaced list format
   - Example:
   ```
   (1) Alprazolam.............................................................................2882
   (2) Barbital...................................................................................2145
   (3) Brexanolone...........................................................................2400
   ```

5. **Sub-items under definitions** - Format: (1), (2), (A), (B), etc.
   - Blank line after each sub-item ONLY if it's a complete paragraph
   - No blank line if it's part of a list
   - Example with blank lines (definitions):
   ```
   (1) A practitioner or pursuant to the lawful direction of a practitioner; or
   
   (2) the patient or research subject at the direction...
   ```
   
   Example without blank lines (simple lists):
   ```
   (2) "Controlled substance analog" does not include:

   (A) A controlled substance;

   (B) a substance for which there is an approved new drug application; or

   (C) a substance with respect to which an exemption is in effect...
   ```

6. **Paragraph Continuity**:
   - Join wrapped lines within same paragraph/sentence
   - Remove artificial line breaks from PDF wrapping
   - Maintain natural paragraph breaks (blank lines between distinct sections)

7. **Spacing Rules Summary**:
   - Section-level definitions (a), (b), (c): BLANK LINE after each
   - Main sections (Sec. 1, Sec. 2): BLANK LINE before, NO blank line after
   - Subsection headings with colons: BLANK LINE before, NO blank line after
   - Numbered drug lists (1), (2), (3): NO blank lines between items (single-spaced)
   - Sub-items in definitions: BLANK LINE after if paragraph-style, NO blank line if list-style

PUNCTUATION RULES:
- Double hyphens "--" must stay as "--" (do not convert to em dash "—")
- Dots for alignment (e.g., "Alprazolam.............2882") → preserve exactly
- Spaces after punctuation: single space after periods, colons, semicolons
