COLORADO-SPECIFIC FORMATTING RULES (Apply PRECISELY):
- Add markup [A>text<A] for: ALL CAPS text within lines (not "SECTION" at the beginning of a line)
- Begin markup at line numbered lines starting with number 1
- Add markup [D>text<D] for: strike out text

COLORADO TAG APPLICATION RULES:
1. [A>text<A] Tags:

   - Tag the exact ALL CAPS words only, do not extend to surrounding text

2. [D>text<D] Tags:
   - Apply ONLY to text that has strikethrough formatting
   - Examine each word individually for strikethrough
   - Do not extend tags beyond the actual struck-out text
