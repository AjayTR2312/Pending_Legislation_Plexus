CALIFORNIA LEGISLATIVE FORMATTING RULES (CRITICAL):
===================================================================

1. **Page 1 Header Section** (PRESERVE ALL OF THIS):
   - **CAPITALIZATION RULE**: The legislative session header MUST be in ALL CAPITAL LETTERS
     * Pattern: "CALIFORNIA LEGISLATURE—[YEAR]–[YEAR] REGULAR SESSION" or
     * Pattern: "SENATE, CALIFORNIA LEGISLATURE—[YEAR]–[YEAR] REGULAR SESSION" or  
     * Pattern: "ASSEMBLY, CALIFORNIA LEGISLATURE—[YEAR]–[YEAR] REGULAR SESSION"
     * Example: "senate, california legislature—2025–26 regular session" → "SENATE, CALIFORNIA LEGISLATURE—2025–26 REGULAR SESSION"
     * Example: "california legislature—2024–25 regular session" → "CALIFORNIA LEGISLATURE—2024–25 REGULAR SESSION"
     * This applies to ANY year combination (2023–24, 2025–26, etc.)
   - Amendment status line (if present)
   - Bill number and type
   - Author and coauthors
   - Date introduced
   - Bill purpose ("An act to...")
   - LEGISLATIVE COUNSEL'S DIGEST
   - Vote/appropriation/fiscal committee information
   - Example:
   ```
   SENATE, CALIFORNIA LEGISLATURE—2025–26 REGULAR SESSION
   
   AMENDED IN SENATE MAY 29, 2025
   
   SENATE BILL No. 509
   
   Introduced by Senator Caballero
   (Coauthors: Assembly Members...)
   
   February 21, 2025
   
   An act to add Section 8588.13...
   ```

2. **Enacting Clause**:
   - Blank line before enacting clause
   - Example:
   ```
   ...from the office.
   
   The people of the State of California do enact as follows:
   ```

3. **Section Headings** - Format: "SECTION 1.", "SEC. 2.", etc.
   - Blank line before section heading
   - No blank line after section heading (flows into text)
   - Example:
   ```
   (previous content)
   
   SECTION 1. The Legislature hereby finds and declares...
   ```

4. **Legislative Findings**:
   - Format: "SECTION 1. The Legislature hereby finds and declares all of the following:"
   - Each finding as lettered item: (a), (b), (c)
   - Blank line after each finding
   - Example:
   ```
   SECTION 1. The Legislature hereby finds and declares all of the following:
   
   (a) California is home to...
   
   (b) The state has...
   ```

5. **Code Sections**:
   - Format: "Section 8588.13 is added to the Government Code, to read:"
   - Subsections with numbers: (a), (b), (c)
   - Sub-items with numbers: (1), (2), (3)
   - Blank line after each subsection
   - Example:
   ```
   8588.13. (a) The office shall do all of the following:
   
   (1) First item here...
   
   (2) Second item here...
   
   (b) Next subsection...
   ```

6. **Numbered/Lettered Lists**:
   - Each item on its own line
   - BLANK LINE after each item
   - Example:
   ```
   (a) The office shall develop...
   
   (b) The office shall coordinate...
   
   (c) The office shall provide...
   ```

7. **Paragraph Continuity**:
   - Join wrapped lines within same paragraph/sentence
   - Remove artificial line breaks from PDF wrapping (text wrapped at ~50-80 characters)
   - Maintain natural paragraph breaks (blank lines between distinct sections)
   - Example:
     * WRONG: "Culturally competent outreach to impacted\ndiaspora communities"
     * RIGHT: "Culturally competent outreach to impacted diaspora communities"

8. **Spacing Rules Summary**:
   - Section headings (SECTION 1, SEC. 2): BLANK LINE before, NO blank line after
   - Lettered items (a), (b), (c): BLANK LINE after each
   - Numbered items (1), (2), (3): BLANK LINE after each
   - Enacting clause: BLANK LINE before "The people of the State of California..."
