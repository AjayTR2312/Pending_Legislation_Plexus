FLORIDA FORMATTING CONVENTIONS:
- Section format: Apply tags exactly as specified in summary
  - Example: [A>Section 1.<A] or [D>old text<D] [A>new text<A]
  
- Multiple tags on same line: Each tag is separate
  - CORRECT: "text [A>word1<A] text [D>word2<D] text [A>word3<A]"
  
- Paragraph separation: Use separate tags for each paragraph
  - CORRECT: [A>para1<A]\n\n[A>para2<A]

- Single characters and punctuation get their own tags if specified:
  - Examples: [A>5<A], [A>a<A], [A>(<A], [A>)<A]

CONTENT PRESERVATION RULES:

CONTENT PRESERVATION WHILE TAGGING (CRITICAL)
===================================================================

ABSOLUTE RULE: Tags are ADDITIVE markup, never REPLACEMENTS

Correct example:
- Input: "word1 word2 word3"
- After tagging word2: "word1 [A>word2<A] word3" ✓ (all words present)

Wrong example:
- Input: "word1 word2 word3"  
- After tagging word2: "word1 [A>word2<A]" ✗ (word3 deleted - FAILURE!)

COMMON MISTAKES AND HOW TO AVOID THEM:

Mistake #1: Stopping after tag instead of continuing
```
INPUT: "An act relating to trusts; amending s. 736.04117, F.S.; revising the definition"
WRONG: "An act relating to trusts; amending s. 736.04117, [A>F.S.<A]" ✗ 
       (stopped after tag, dropped "; revising the definition")
CORRECT: "An act relating to trusts; amending s. 736.04117, [A>F.S.<A]; revising the definition" ✓
```

Mistake #2: Dropping text at end of line
```
INPUT: "must shall grant the petition unless the court finds good cause"
WRONG: "must [D>shall<D] grant the petition" ✗
       (dropped everything after "petition")
CORRECT: "must [D>shall<D] grant the petition unless the court finds good cause" ✓
```

Mistake #3: Forgetting text after mid-sentence tag
```
INPUT: "otherwise, an authorized trustee who has absolute power under the terms"
WRONG: "otherwise, an [A>authorized<A] trustee" ✗
       (forgot "who has absolute power under the terms")
CORRECT: "otherwise, an [A>authorized<A] trustee who has absolute power under the terms" ✓
```

VERIFICATION RULE: After inserting ANY tag, always check:
- Are all words that came BEFORE the tag still there? (Check left side)
