HOUSE BILL 1375

A Bill

State of Arkansas

95th General Assembly

Regular Session, 2025

By: Representatives Pilkington, J. Richardson

By: Senator K. Hammer

For An Act To Be Entitled

AN ACT TO REQUIRE A REPORT CONCERNING FUNDS SUPPORTING ORGANIZATIONS OR PROGRAMS FOR INDIVIDUALS STARTING NEW BUSINESSES OR BUSINESSES ESTABLISHED WITHIN THE PREVIOUS FIVE YEARS; AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.

Subtitle

TO REQUIRE A REPORT CONCERNING FUNDS SUPPORTING PROGRAMS FOR INDIVIDUALS STARTING NEW BUSINESSES OR BUSINESSES ESTABLISHED WITHIN THE PREVIOUS FIVE YEARS.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ARKANSAS:

SECTION 1. Arkansas Code Title 25, Chapter 43, Subchapter 3, is amended to add an additional section to read as follows:

[A>25-43-306. Report concerning funds supporting organizations or programs for individuals starting new businesses or businesses established within the previous five years.<A]

[A>(a) By January 1 of each year the Secretary of the Department of Commerce shall file an annual report that includes without limitation:<A]

[A>(1) The percentage of workforce development funding, including without limitation funding allocated by workforce development boards across the state, supporting organizations or programs:<A]

[A>(A) For individuals starting new businesses in this state; or<A]

[A>(B) That provide services to businesses established within the previous five (5) years whose principal place of business is in this state; and<A]

[A>(2) The total dollar amount of workforce development funding, including without limitation funding allocated by workforce development boards across the state, supporting organizations or programs:<A]

[A>(A) For individuals starting new businesses in this state; or<A]

[A>(B) That provide services to businesses established within the previous five (5) years whose principal place of business is in this state.<A]

[A>(b) The annual report under subsection (a) of this section shall be filed with the:<A]

[A>(1) Governor;<A]

[A>(2) House Committee on Insurance and Commerce; and<A]

[A>(3) Senate Committee on Insurance and Commerce.<A]

SECTION 2. DO NOT CODIFY. TEMPORARY LANGUAGE. [A>The first annual report under § 25-43-306 is due by January 1, 2027.<A]
