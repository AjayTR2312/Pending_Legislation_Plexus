HOUSE BILL 1376

A Bill

State of Arkansas

95th General Assembly

Regular Session, 2025

By: Representatives Pilkington, J. Richardson

By: Senator K. Hammer

For An Act To Be Entitled

AN ACT TO ENCOURAGE STATE AGENCIES TO AWARD A PORTION OF STATE CONTRACTS TO ARKANSAS BUSINESSES THAT HAVE BEEN IN OPERATION FOR LESS THAN FIVE YEARS; AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.

Subtitle

TO ENCOURAGE STATE AGENCIES TO AWARD A PORTION OF STATE CONTRACTS TO ARKANSAS BUSINESSES THAT HAVE BEEN IN OPERATION FOR LESS THAN FIVE YEARS.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ARKANSAS:

SECTION 1. Arkansas Code Title 19, Chapter 11, Subchapter 1, is amended to add an additional section to read as follows:

[A>19-11-108. Award of contracts to Arkansas businesses in operation less than five years -- Definition.<A]

[A>(a) As used in this section, "state agency" means the same as defined under § 19-11-203.<A]

[A>(b) The Office of State Procurement shall encourage each state agency to award at least five percent (5%) of contracts entered into by the state agency to businesses:<A]

[A>(1) That have been in operation for less than five (5) years; and<A]

[A>(2) Whose principal place of business is in this state.<A]
