HOUSE BILL NO. HB0253

Construction lien revisions.

Sponsored by: Representative(s) Wylie, Byron, Connolly, Davis, Erickson, Geringer, Larsen, L, Larson, JT, Nicholas, Provenza, Schmid, Tarver and Wharff and Senator(s) Anderson and Cooper

A BILL

for

AN ACT relating to liens; revising requirements for preliminary lien notices for contractors, subcontractors and materialmen; and providing for an effective date.

Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Wyoming:

Section 1. W.S. 29-2-112(a)(ii)(B) and (iii) is amended to read:

29-2-112. Preliminary notices.

(a) With respect to perfecting the right to file a construction lien under this chapter, the following preliminary notice requirements shall apply:

(ii) Any notice required under this section shall be sent:

(B) By the subcontractor or materialman [D>within thirty (30) days after first providing services or materials to the construction project <D][A>before final payment by the owner to the general contractor<A].

(iii) [A>Except as otherwise provided in this paragraph, f<A]ailure to send the notice required under this section within the time specified shall bar the right of a contractor, subcontractor or materialman to assert a lien[A>. A subcontractor or materialman shall not be barred from asserting a lien under this paragraph if the contractor failed to provide the information required under W.S. 29-2-113 to the subcontractor or materialman<A];

Section 2. This act is effective July 1, 2025.

(END)
