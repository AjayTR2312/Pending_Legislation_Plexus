LEGISLATURE OF NEBRASKA

ONE HUNDRED NINTH LEGISLATURE

FIRST SESSION

LEGISLATIVE BILL 608A

Introduced by Bostar, 29.

Read first time May 09, 2025

A BILL FOR AN ACT relating to appropriations; to appropriate funds to aid in carrying out the provisions of Legislative Bill 608, One Hundred Ninth Legislature, First Session, 2025.

Be it enacted by the people of the State of Nebraska,

Section 1. [A>There is hereby appropriated (1) $781,647 from the General Fund for FY2025-26 and (2) $1,351,495 from the General Fund for FY2026-27 to the Coordinating Commission for Postsecondary Education, for Program 692, to aid in carrying out the provisions of Legislative Bill 608, One Hundred Ninth Legislature, First Session, 2025.<A]

[A>There is included in the appropriation to this program for FY2025-26 $781,647 General Funds for state aid, which shall only be used for such purpose. There is included in the appropriation to this program for FY2026-27 $1,351,495 General Funds for state aid, which shall only be used for such purpose.<A]

[A>No expenditures for permanent and temporary salaries and per diems for state employees shall be made from funds appropriated in this section.<A]
