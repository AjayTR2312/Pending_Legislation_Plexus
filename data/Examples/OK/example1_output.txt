1st Session of the 60th Legislature (2025)

HOUSE BILL 1573

By: Lawson

An Act relating to the Department of Human Services; defining term; directing the Department to carry out certain task; and providing an effective date.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE PEOPLE OF THE STATE OF OKLAHOMA:

SECTION 1. NEW LAW A new section of law to be codified in the Oklahoma Statutes as Section 151 of Title 56, unless there is created a duplication in numbering, reads as follows:

A. For purposes of this section, "sensitive compartmented information facility" means an area, room, group of rooms, or installation where confidential information may be viewed, stored, used, discussed, or electronically processed and where procedural and physical measures prevent the free access of persons unless such persons are authorized by the Department of Human Services to access such facility.

B. The Department of Human Services shall direct, manage, and oversee the building and maintenance of a sensitive compartmented information facility.

SECTION 2. This act shall become effective November 1, 2025.
