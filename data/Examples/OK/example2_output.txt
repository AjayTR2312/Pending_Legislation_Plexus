An Act relating to children; amending 10 O.S. 2021, Section 601.6, which relates to the Office of Juvenile System Oversight; providing reference for definition; directing the Office to inspect misfeasance and malfeasance; directing the Office to conduct inspections annually; authorizing the Office to inspect certain privately-operated facilities; directing the Office to investigate complaints; authorizing the Office to examine and copy records and budgets; authorizing the Office to interview certain individuals; authorizing the Office to subpoena witnesses and hold hearings; and declaring an emergency.
