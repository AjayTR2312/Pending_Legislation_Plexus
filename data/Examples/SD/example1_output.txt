2025 South Dakota Legislature

House Bill 1080

HOUSE JUDICIARY ENGROSSED

Introduced by: Representative Peterson (Drew)

An Act to void covenants that prohibit or restrict the possession or use of firearms and ammunition.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF SOUTH DAKOTA:

Section 1. That a NEW SECTION be added to chapter 43-12:

[A>A covenant running with the land may not prohibit, restrict, or have the effect of prohibiting or restricting:<A]

[A>(1) The lawful possession, storage, or transportation of a firearm, any part of a firearm, or ammunition; or<A]

[A>(2) The lawful discharge of a firearm.<A]

[A>If a provision in a covenant is found to violate this section, that provision is void and unenforceable. A provision that is void and unenforceable is severable and any remaining provisions that can be given effect are not thereby affected.<A]
