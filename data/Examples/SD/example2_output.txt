2025 South Dakota Legislature

House Bill 1201

Introduced by: Representative Manhart

An Act to establish requirements for school counseling services.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF SOUTH DAKOTA:

Section 1. That a NEW SECTION be added to chapter 13-32:

[A>Before a school counselor provides a direct counseling service to a student for the first time, the school counselor, or another school staff member, must obtain the consent of the student's parent or guardian.<A]

[A>For the purpose of sections 1 and 2 of this Act, "direct counseling service" means an interaction, between a school counselor and a student, pertaining to mental, medical, or emotional health and does not involve career planning or academic discussions.<A]

Section 2. That a NEW SECTION be added to chapter 13-32:

[A>A school counselor, or another school staff member, must notify a student's parent or guardian:<A]

[A>(1) When a direct counseling service is provided to a student;<A]

[A>(2) If a subject is discussed during a direct counseling service that is substantially different from those discussed during previous counseling sessions; and<A]

[A>(3) If questions regarding any of the following topics are discussed with a student during a direct counseling service:<A]

[A>(a) Gender;<A]

[A>(b) Gender confusion;<A]

[A>(c) Gender dysphoria;<A]

[A>(d) Homosexuality; or<A]

[A>(e) Transsexuality.<A]

Section 3. That a NEW SECTION be added to chapter 13-32:

[A>School counselors may not promote the concept that a student is a sex contrary to the student's biological sex at birth.<A]
