Resolve, to Rename a Bridge in Bingham the Barry A. DeLong Bridge

Received by the Clerk of the House on January 30, 2025. Referred to the Committee on Transportation pursuant to Joint Rule 308.2 and ordered printed pursuant to Joint Rule 401.

ROBERT B. HUNT

Clerk

Presented by Representative DUCHARME of Madison.

Cosponsored by Senator FARRIN of Somerset and

Representatives: CARUSO of Caratunk, POIRIER of Skowhegan, UNDERWOOD of Presque Isle, Senator: BLACK of Franklin.

Sec. 1. Bridge in Bingham renamed. Resolved: That the Department of Transportation shall designate Bridge 3236 on Bridge Street in the Town of Bingham, which crosses the Kennebec River and is currently known as the Kennebec River Bridge, the Barry A. DeLong Bridge.

SUMMARY

This resolve directs the Department of Transportation to designate Bridge 3236 on Bridge Street in the Town of Bingham the Barry A. DeLong Bridge.
