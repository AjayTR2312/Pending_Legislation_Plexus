AN ACT CONCERNING THE ADOPTION OF MUNICIPAL CONFLICT OF INTEREST PROVISIONS.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Assembly convened:

Section 1. (NEW) (Effective October 1, 2025) (a) As used in this section, (1) "municipality" means each town, city or borough, whether consolidated or unconsolidated, and includes any district, and (2) "district" has the same meaning as provided in section 7-324 of the general statutes. Notwithstanding any provision of the general statutes or any special act, municipal charter or ordinance, not later than October 1, 2026, each municipality shall adopt a conflict of interest provision for its elected and appointed officials, officers and employees which shall contain: (A) The municipality's definition of "conflict of interest" or the model definition set forth in subsection (b) of this section, and (B) any actions that are required to be taken by the municipality if a conflict of interest is determined to exist, except that the provisions of this subsection shall not apply to any municipality that has established a conflict of interest policy prior to January 1, 2026.

(b) For purposes of complying with the requirements of subsection (a) of this section, a municipality may adopt the following model definition of conflict of interest in lieu of creating its own definition: A conflict of interest exists where an official, officer or employee believes or expects that such official, officer or employee, or any person within such official's, officer's or employee's immediate family, or any business associate of such official, officer or employee, or any business by which the official, officer or employee is employed, or any business with which the official, officer or employee is associated, will derive a direct monetary gain or suffer a direct monetary loss, as the case may be, by reason of such official's, officer's or employee's official activity, but excluding any official action taken by the official, officer or employee in which any benefit or detriment is shared with a substantial segment of the population of the municipality.

(c) Not later than January 15, 2027, each municipality shall submit a notice to the Office of State Ethics, in a manner prescribed by the office, certifying whether the municipality (1) has adopted a conflict of interest provision in accordance with the provisions of subsection (a) of this section, or (2) is exempt from such requirement under subsection (a) of this section. Any such notice shall include a copy of the conflict of interest provision adopted by such municipality. The Office of State Ethics shall publish a copy of each municipality's conflict of interest provision submitted under this subsection on the office's Internet web site.

(d) Not later than January 15, 2027, and as updated thereafter, each municipality shall publish any conflict of interest provision adopted by such municipality on its Internet web site in an easily accessible location. If the conflict of interest provision is part of an established code of ethics adopted by a municipality, the publication of the code of ethics on the municipal Internet web site in an easily accessible location shall satisfy the requirements of this subsection. If the municipality does not have an Internet web site, the municipality shall make its conflict of interest provision or code of ethics available to the public at the town or city clerk's office.

This act shall take effect as follows and shall amend the following sections:

Section 1                 October 1, 2025                         New section

Statement of Purpose:

To require municipalities that do not have an adopted conflict of interest provision to adopt such a provision and all municipalities to submit a copy of such provision to the Office of State Ethics and post such provision on their Internet web site.
